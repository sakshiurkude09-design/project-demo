Solar Panels

Uses of Solar Panels

  • Residential Electricity: Power homes with solar energy to reduce electricity bills.
  • Commercial & Industrial: Supply electricity to factories, offices, and malls.
  • Solar Water Heating: Heating water for homes, swimming pools, or industries.
  • Solar Street Lights: Power street lamps without connecting to the grid.
  • Remote Areas: Provide electricity in off-grid areas or villages.
  • Agriculture: Run irrigation pumps, greenhouses, and farm equipment.
  • Transportation: Used in solar-powered cars, boats, and planes.

Advantages of Solar Panels

  • Eco-friendly: Clean energy with no greenhouse gas emissions.
  • Cost-saving: Reduce electricity bills and sell excess power.
  • Low Maintenance: Lasts 25+ years with minimal maintenance.
  • Energy Independence: Less dependency on fossil fuels.
  • Versatile Installation: Rooftops, ground mounts, and mobile systems.
  • Increases Property Value: Homes with solar panels have higher value.

Qualities / Features to Look For

  • Efficiency: 15–22% sunlight conversion efficiency.
  • Durability: Withstands rain, heat, and harsh weather.
  • Warranty: Usually 20–25 years.
  • Material: Monocrystalline or Polycrystalline.
  • Temperature Coefficient: Better performance in high temperatures.
